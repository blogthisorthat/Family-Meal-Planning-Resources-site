What this tool does

  • Reads public Recipe JSON-LD when the source site provides it.
  • Detects the recipe’s serving yield when available.
  • Scales common whole numbers, decimals, fractions, mixed fractions, and quantity ranges.
  • Lets you correct the original serving count before scaling.
  • Adds the imported recipe to a selected day in the weekly planner.
Important: Ingredient scaling is mathematical, not culinary judgment. Baking recipes, spices, thickeners, cooking times, pan sizes, and some sauces may not scale perfectly linearly. Review the result before cooking.

Recipe scaling help

Why can’t every recipe URL be imported?

Some recipe sites do not publish their ingredients as standard structured data, block automated requests, require a login, or build the recipe entirely in the browser. In those cases the importer may not be able to read the recipe.

Does the tool copy the whole recipe?

No. It extracts the ingredient list and basic recipe name/yield needed for scaling. The original recipe page remains the source for instructions, photos, notes, and preparation details.

Can I add the result to my grocery list?

Yes. Choose a day and use “Add to Meal Planner.” The scaled ingredients are saved into the same browser-based meal plan used by the weekly planner.

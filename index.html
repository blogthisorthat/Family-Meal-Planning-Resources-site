The easy rule

Pick five dinners, plan one leftover night, and leave one night flexible. That's usually enough structure without over-planning.

A simple meal-planning system

Three steps you can repeat every week.

🧊

1. Shop your kitchen

Check the freezer, fridge, and pantry first. Build at least one meal around food you already own.

🗓️

2. Choose five dinners

Mix easy favorites with one or two new ideas. Keep the busiest night especially simple.

🛒

3. Make one list

Group the missing ingredients by store section so you spend less time wandering and impulse buying.

Ways to stretch the grocery budget

Simple habits that work without making every meal feel cheap.

Plan around proteins on sale

Decide your meals after checking the week's deals instead of choosing expensive proteins first.

Use one ingredient twice

A roast chicken can become sandwiches, tacos, soup, or rice bowls later in the week.

Keep backup meals

Eggs, pasta, quesadillas, or freezer meals can stop an exhausting day from turning into an expensive takeout night.
